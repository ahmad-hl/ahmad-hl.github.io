Aalto University
2025 – present
Real‑time Rendering & Streaming of Photorealistic 3D Views
Leveraging 3D Gaussian Splatting and generative scene representations for real-time novel view synthesis. We design adaptive streaming pipelines that deliver photorealistic immersive scenes to lightweight XR headsets with minimal bandwidth.

Adaptive Bitrate Streaming for XR Cloud Gaming
Developing low-latency adaptive bitrate algorithms and congestion control for immersive XR cloud gaming. Focus on integrating gaze-driven foveated transport and real-time encoding optimizations for stable quality under fluctuating network conditions.
